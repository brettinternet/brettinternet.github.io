My [TRMNL recipes](https://github.com/brettinternet/trmnl) put useful
information on small displays around the house. The e-ink screens are subtle and
blend in well with a room. They show weather forecasts, historical graphs, a
Home Assistant family calendar, a Stoic calendar for days left in the year,
daily comics, featured Wikipedia articles, current events, and even images.

I run [LaraPaper](https://github.com/usetrmnl/larapaper), a self-hosted TRMNL
server. I've flashed several unofficial devices with the TRMNL firmware.
